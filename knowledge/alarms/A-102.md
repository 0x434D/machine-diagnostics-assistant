# A-102 · Feeder lane 2 empty

**Station** S1 Feeding · **Severity** fault · S1 cannot present a lane-2 component.

Identical in mechanism to A-101, on the other lane. See A-101 for the full reading.

## Why the lanes are alarmed separately

Because lane identity is diagnostic. Defects concentrating on one lane point at what that
lane carries or how it presents it (DP-03), and a lane that repeatedly empties while the
other does not is a fact about that lane's supply, not about the line.

When both A-101 and A-102 stand together, treat it as one supply event, not two faults.

See also: S1, DP-03, DP-07.
