# A-303 · Vision system not responding

**Station** S3 Inspection · **Severity** fault · the vision system did not return a
classification within the cycle.

## What it means

The camera belongs to the station; the vision system is a separate box beside it. This alarm
is about that box — it did not answer. The image may well have been captured fine.

## Check

1. Whether images were being acquired at the same time (A-301 standing or not). Acquisition
   fine plus no classification isolates the fault to the vision system.
2. Whether it is a timeout or a total loss of response. A timeout under load and an
   unreachable box are different problems.
3. The model version on the last results before the alarm, and on the first after — a change
   there means the population either side is not directly comparable.

## What it does not mean

Nothing about part quality. Parts that passed S3 without a verdict have **unknown**
disposition and must be reported as unknown.

See also: S3, A-301.
