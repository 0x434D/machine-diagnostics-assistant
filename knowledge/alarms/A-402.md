# A-402 · Reject chute full

**Station** S4 Outfeed · **Severity** fault · rejected parts cannot be discharged.

## What it means

S4 cannot sort, so the line stops. The cause is the **handling of scrap**, not scrap itself —
but the two are linked, and that link is the useful part.

## The reading worth making

A reject chute filling faster than usual is a scrap-rate signal arriving through a mechanical
route. If this alarm appears earlier in a shift than it normally does, ask what the reject
rate was doing, because the chute filling early is a consequence of producing more rejects.

If the rate is normal, then the chute simply was not emptied, and it is a housekeeping stop.

## Check

1. Reject count and scrap rate over the shift, against baseline.
2. When the chute was last cleared, if known.
3. Whether a defect class dominates the rejects — a full chute plus one dominant class is a
   quality investigation (SOP-02) with a stop attached.

## What it does not mean

It does not, by itself, mean quality got worse. Check the rate before drawing that conclusion.

See also: S4, SOP-02.
