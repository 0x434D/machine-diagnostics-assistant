# A-101 · Feeder lane 1 empty

**Station** S1 Feeding · **Severity** fault · S1 cannot present a lane-1 component.

## What it means

Supply to lane 1 has run out. `LaneFill_1` will have fallen to zero ahead of the alarm — the
fill signal is the leading indicator and the alarm is the confirmation.

This is the **head of the line**. There is no upstream station, so a stop that terminates
here is `external_upstream`: nothing on this line caused it and nothing on this line will
clear it.

## Check

1. `LaneFill_1` over the preceding period — did it fall steadily, or drop abruptly? Steady
   decline is consumption outrunning replenishment; an abrupt drop is a supply interruption.
2. Whether lane 2 fell at the same time. Both lanes means the supply to the line; one lane
   means that lane.
3. Whether a lot change coincides — a lane that emptied at a lot boundary may simply have
   finished a lot.

## What it does not mean

It does not mean the line is broken, and it is not a reason to investigate S2, S3 or S4 —
they will all be `Suspended(starved)` in sequence, which is a consequence and not a finding.

See also: S1, DP-07, SOP-01.
