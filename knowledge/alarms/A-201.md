# A-201 · Joining distance out of tolerance

**Station** S2 Joining · **Severity** fault · the press travelled outside its distance window.

## What it means

The press reached a final position it should not have. **Distance is the more informative of
the two recorded scalars for a material problem**: undersized components let the press travel
further while the peak force looks entirely normal, because force is reached against a
different geometry.

So A-201 without A-207 — distance out, force fine — leans towards **material**, and A-207
without A-201 leans towards the **press**. That is a lean, not a conclusion. Settle it with the
**force–distance curve** (S2): a contact point that has moved means the components changed; a
changed shape after contact means the press did.

## Check

1. Which direction the distance went: further than nominal, or short.
2. The force–distance curve for the affected parts, and whether the peak moved with it.
3. **What the affected parts share** — lot, lane, carrier. Lot correlation settles what the
   two scalars can only suggest.
4. Gauge a pulled part. A distance fault and a real dimensional problem should agree.

## What it does not mean

A single part out of tolerance is a single part. Check whether the surrounding parts moved
before calling it a drift.

See also: S2, A-207, `gap`, DP-01, DP-05.
