# A-401 · Outfeed full

**Station** S4 Outfeed · **Severity** fault · finished parts are not being taken away.

## What it means

The **tail of the line**. There is no downstream station, so a chain that terminates here is
`external_downstream`: nothing on this line is at fault. `OutfeedFill` rising towards capacity
is the leading indicator; the alarm is the confirmation.

## Check

1. `OutfeedFill` over the preceding period — how long the rise took tells you whether removal
   slowed or stopped outright.
2. How far back the blockage propagated. It will walk upstream until the whole line stands.
3. Whether anything on the line changed at the same time. Usually nothing did, and saying so
   is the finding.

## What it does not mean

A whole line standing looks systemic and is not. Report the category, not the scale of the
disruption — the maintainer's next action is to go and find out why parts stopped being taken
away, and every minute spent examining S1 through S3 is wasted.

See also: S4, DP-08, SOP-01.
