# A-202 · Press did not reach home position

**Station** S2 Joining · **Severity** fault · the press did not return to its start position
within the cycle, so S2 cannot begin the next one.

## What it means

A mechanical condition at the press itself, not a judgement about any part. S2 stops and needs
an operator, which makes it a **cause candidate** in a stop chain — and a genuine one, since
nothing downstream can cause a press not to retract.

## Check

1. Whether a part is jammed in the press (A-900 may stand alongside).
2. The last part processed before the alarm — its force and distance are the closest evidence
   to the moment the press failed to return.
3. Whether this recurs. A repeating home-position fault is a machine condition; a single one
   after a jam is a consequence of the jam.

## What it does not mean

It says nothing about the quality of parts already produced. Do not fold it into a scrap
investigation unless the parts themselves show something.

See also: S2, A-900, SOP-01.
