# A-301 · Image acquisition failed

**Station** S3 Inspection · **Severity** fault · the camera did not deliver an image for a
part.

## What it means

No image, therefore no verdict. This is a **camera** failure, distinct from the vision system
failing to classify (A-303) and from the imaging degrading while still working (A-302).

## Check

1. Whether parts continued through S3 without results. Parts inspected without a verdict are
   an unknown-disposition population and must be reported as such, not as good.
2. Whether it is one part or a run. A single failed acquisition is a nuisance; a run is a
   station fault.
3. Trigger and part presence — an acquisition that fired with no part is a different fault
   from one that fired and got nothing.

## What it does not mean

It is not a quality signal. Do not let acquisition failures enter a defect rate in either
direction: they are neither good parts nor rejects, and quietly counting them as either
distorts the window.

See also: S3, A-302, A-303.
